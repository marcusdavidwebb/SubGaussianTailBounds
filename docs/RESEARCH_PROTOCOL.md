# Mathematical research and attribution

Treat correctness, attribution and novelty separately. Neither Lean nor hybrid
verification establishes priority or completeness of the literature assessment.

Before substantial proof development, state the target and hypotheses and
investigate relevant primary sources, including stronger results and the sources
of the proof methods. Search alternative mathematical formulations.

For material comparisons record the primary source/version, exact theorem/page,
what was inspected, hypotheses and the precise implication or obstruction.
An abstract or snippet is not full-text verification. Do not invent metadata
or imply that inaccessible sources were read.

Distinguish established results, reformulations, adaptations, alternative proofs,
candidate new results and unresolved assessments. Attribute borrowed arguments
where used. Independent derivation and unsuccessful searches do not establish
novelty. Preserve unresolved attribution/novelty markers until evidence resolves
them.

Each substantive contribution's concise explanation and Lean documentation
should record its literature comparison, access limitations, checks and
outstanding questions. Do not require or maintain a separate prose proof.
The maintainer reviews substantive statement and attribution changes.
