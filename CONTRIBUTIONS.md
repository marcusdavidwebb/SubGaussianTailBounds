# Contributions

## Initial contribution — 3 November 2026

Marcus Webb contributed the initial results for the general and unit regimes,
including the general quartet `(96/25, 79/50, 47/20, 3/2)`. He is the sole
author of the historical supplement and takes full responsibility for it; its
proof and exposition were AI-generated without his direct input. The accompanying
paper is by Marcus Webb and Morgan Lynch-Davies, whose preceding mathematical
work yielded weaker bounds.

Codex (GPT-6), parallel GPT-6.1 Sol Ultra workers and a coordinator generated
candidate mathematics, code and exposition. Lean checked proof terms, Python
performed external exact arithmetic, and Wolfram supported scalar exploration
without supplying accepted mathematical facts. Model review is not independent
human review, and paper authorship is a separate decision.

The original hybrid verification checked the analytic implication in Lean and
finite conditions in Python; it did not supply complete canonical Lean proofs.
Complete Lean proofs of the general and unit results followed. Attribution and
novelty assessments remain unresolved, including the selector/Gaussian
spectral-limit discussion and the sparse example recorded as inherited from
Jeong–Li–Plan–Yılmaz. See the [archived provenance](https://github.com/marcusdavidwebb/SubGaussianTailBounds/blob/480fa4b70f12c1362c88c7e05abce1a853f91bb9/docs/PROVENANCE.md)
for details.
